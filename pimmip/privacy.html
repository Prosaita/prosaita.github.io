Pim & Mip Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Pim & Mip is a puzzle game for Android published by Prosaita Studios. This page explains what information the game handles. In short: the game does not collect, send or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The game does not ask you to create an account, does not request your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other data, and does not use advertising identifiers.

What is stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game stores the following only on your own device: completed levels, flowers earned, available hints and settings (sound, music, language and text size). This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

Internet access, ads and analytics

The current version of the game does not connect to the internet, does not show third-party ads and does not include analytics or tracking tools. In-app purchases are not active.

If a future version adds ads or in-app purchases, this policy will be updated before that version is released, explaining what data those services handle and what choices you have.

Children

The game is intended for people aged 13 and over and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Since we do not process any of your personal data, we hold no data to access, correct or delete. If you have any questions, please contact us.
